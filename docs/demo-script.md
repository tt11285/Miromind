# ValuationLens Three-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 to 0:20 - Problem

Financial research often gives users a polished conclusion without showing how the AI moved from the question to hypotheses, evidence, counter-evidence, uncertainty, and judgment. ValuationLens solves that by making the research process transparent and auditable.

## 0:20 to 0:45 - Setup

Search for `NVIDIA` or `NVDA`, select `NVIDIA Corporation | NVDA | NASDAQ | US | Equity`, then run the question:

> Is NVIDIA's current valuation justified by AI growth fundamentals?

Point out that the company is selected from a listed-security dropdown, so the agent does not guess the ticker. Microsoft, Micron, and Tesla are also available in the same selector, with Micron as the secondary demo path.

## 0:45 to 1:20 - Workflow

ValuationLens is not a single report-generation prompt. After clicking Run Deep Research, the UI streams the staged workflow and exposes reviewable artifacts: task frame, hypothesis tree, evidence plan, evidence cards, scoring, reasoning notes, and the memo.

## 1:20 to 2:05 - Hypothesis Tree

The system converts the NVIDIA question into a hypothesis tree. The root question branches into node-level claims about AI revenue durability, margin resilience, supply constraints, customer concentration, competitive pressure, and valuation sensitivity. Open Demand Sustainability and Valuation Sensitivity. Show the supporting evidence, counter-evidence, confidence, and reasoning notes for each node. Evidence cards attach to the relevant nodes so a reviewer can see what supports each claim, what challenges it, and where uncertainty remains. Every node keeps both the pro and con case.

## 2:05 to 2:35 - Investment Memo

The final investment memo summarizes the research path and gives the stance: Partially Supported. That means the AI growth fundamentals provide meaningful support for NVIDIA's valuation, but the conclusion still depends on execution, sustained demand, margin durability, and how much future growth is already reflected in the price.

## 2:35 to 3:00 - Reasoning Transparency

The key point is traceability. Click a memo section trace button such as Demand Sustainability or Valuation Sensitivity, and show how the final memo highlights related hypothesis nodes and evidence cards behind the Partially Supported stance. Instead of asking viewers to accept a confident answer, ValuationLens exposes the hypothesis tree, evidence cards, node-level reasoning, uncertainty, and final judgment for review. ValuationLens makes the AI's investment research process traceable, challengeable, and reviewable.
